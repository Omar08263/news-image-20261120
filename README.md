Sum up 20 November's world news in one image: your program writes one image of the day's news, an AI judge compares the images two at a time, and the 50 best win rates share $250: $50 for 1st place, $30 for 2nd, $15 for 3rd, $5 each for 4th to 10th and $3 each for 11th to 50th.

The entry is one editorial poster (1024×1024): the day's biggest story — Russia's mass missile and drone attack on Ukraine, with two missiles and a drone landing in Poland — above three more: Ukraine's liberation of 14 villages in Kharkiv, the AI stock rout, and the 6.7 earthquake in Peru, with the Hormuz war, COP31, the Saudi strikes on Hodeida and NATO's jet scramble in the footer. The date is named in the masthead; every headline is drawn as crisp text so the image reads at any size.
